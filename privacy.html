Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

DealSpot is a static informational site. It does not have user accounts, does not run its own analytics or advertising trackers, and does not set its own cookies.

What data is collected

  • Hosting logs: this site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log standard server data (IP address, browser type, requested page) as part of its own infrastructure. We don't have access to this data beyond what GitHub's own privacy policy describes.
  • Outbound links: clicking a deal takes you to Amazon's own site, where Amazon's privacy policy and cookies apply — not ours.
  • Contact: if you email us, we keep that email only to answer your message.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share any data, because we don't collect any beyond what's described above. There's no newsletter sign-up, no account creation, and no first-party tracking pixel on this site.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, standard GDPR/UK GDPR rights apply to any data a service actually holds about you. Since DealSpot itself doesn't hold personal data outside of emails you send us, requests about your data are best directed to GitHub Pages (as the host) or to Amazon (for anything related to your Amazon account or purchases).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if we add analytics in the future — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.
