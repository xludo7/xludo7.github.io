About DealSpot

DealSpot is a small, independent deals page covering Amazon marketplaces across Italy, Germany, France, Spain, the UK, the Netherlands and Belgium. It's run by one person, not a company.

How deals get picked

Every listing on this site is checked against its own price history before it's published — nothing goes up unless the current price is meaningfully below its recent average. Most of what you see is found and priced automatically through a weekly price-tracking check; the note under each of those cards is generated straight from that price data (for example, how far below the recent average the price currently sits), not copied product description text. A handful of earlier picks also include a short note written by hand.

Prices, discounts and availability change fast on Amazon, so the "updated" date on each card reflects when it was last verified.

How the site makes money

DealSpot is a participant in the Amazon EU Associates Programme. When you click through and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure for details on which marketplaces this currently applies to.
